# Design Reference Brief

- Favor video-first layouts: large media surfaces, thin chrome, compact controls, and no marketing panels in the working view.
- Use a dark studio workspace for analysis with charcoal panels, subtle separators, orange active/progress accents, and restrained borders.
- Keep editing available at all times through compact toolbar groups with icons, short labels, color swatches, and visible playback/sync/save controls.
- Borrow the split-screen feel from the references: clear divider, paired timelines, minimal per-pane headers, and controls close to the video.
- Use library screens as simple thumbnail grids/lists with sparse metadata rather than feature copy.
- Avoid heavy gradients, oversized rounded cards, nested cards, repeated explanatory text, and login/account prompts.
